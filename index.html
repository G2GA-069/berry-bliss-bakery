Our Manifesto

Born from the ashes of boring baking

Dark Crumb was summoned in 2020 from a candlelit basement in Camden Town with one sacred vow: to prove that darkness belongs in the kitchen.

We use activated charcoal, black cocoa, squid ink, and other ingredients the mainstream fears. Every muffin is baked at midnight, because that's when the oven speaks to us. No pastels. No sprinkles. No compromise.

We're not for everyone. We're for the ones who wear black to brunch and think vanilla is a lifestyle choice they refuse to make.
