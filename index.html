Our Story

Born from a love of blueberries

Berry Bliss started in 2019 in a tiny kitchen in South London with one simple idea: make the best blueberry muffin anyone has ever tasted.

We source our blueberries from local farms, use only free-range eggs and real butter, and bake everything fresh before dawn. No shortcuts, no preservatives, no nonsense — just proper muffins made with love.

What started as a weekend market stall is now a neighbourhood favourite. Pop in, grab a muffin, and see what the fuss is about.
